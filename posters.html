Dhanji —
Limited Edition

Dhanji is a DHH (Desi Hip Hop) artist from Ahmedabad whose music hits hard — raw, street-level, and unapologetically bold. When I listened to his tracks, the sound painted a very specific visual in my head: gritty textures, burning orange tones, and a vintage poster feel that matched the ruggedness of his style.

I wanted the poster to sound like his music looks — so I leaned into distressed paper textures, a duotone red-orange treatment on his portrait, and a bold hand-crafted typeface for the name. The repeated “RUAB” watermark adds depth and references his artistic identity. The Limited Edition stamp gives the piece a collectible, physical-poster feel.
